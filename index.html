Frequently Asked Questions

Is EduNova really free?

Yes! EduNova is completely free to start. You can access all courses, lessons, and quizzes without any cost. We believe education should be accessible to everyone.

How do I earn certificates?

Complete all lessons in a course and pass the final quiz with a score of 70% or higher to earn a verifiable certificate.

Can I learn at my own pace?

Absolutely! Our study planner helps you set your own pace. You can access lessons anytime, anywhere, and track your progress.
